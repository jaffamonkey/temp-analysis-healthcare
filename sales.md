# Navigating the Gatekeeper (Receptionist/Assistant)

The goal here is not to sell the gatekeeper, but to position the call as expected, professional, and highly relevant.

**The Script**:

"Hi, I’m hoping you can help me. I am calling from Practical Accessibility Ltd, and I have a custom accessibility report that we ran specifically for [Company Name]'s website.Could you point me toward the person who oversees your digital product or engineering roadmap? It might be your Head of Product or CTO."

## If they ask what it's about**:

"We provide specialist accessibility support for digital healthcare platforms. We recently ran a free, preliminary accessibility test across a few of [Company Name]’s key web pages, and I just need to get this custom dashboard over to the right person so they have a baseline of the results."  

## The "Honest Partner" Approach

**The Script**:

"Hi, I’m hoping you can save me from bouncing around the directory. I’m calling from Practical Accessibility Ltd, and our engineers actually just finished running a custom accessibility baseline report specifically for [Company Name].

I know your leadership team's time is incredibly guarded, and I don't want to blindly try and throw a meeting on anyone's calendar. I was originally trying to figure out if this report belongs with your CTO or the Head of Product, but given how protective you have to be of their time, what is the best way for me to leave this snapshot with you first?

That way, you have the data, and you can decide if it’s worth bringing to their attention right now."

**Why this blend works so well**:

It lowers defenses instantly: By stating you want to avoid "bouncing around the directory" and "blindly throwing a meeting on the calendar," you are speaking the gatekeeper's love language. You are acknowledging their biggest daily annoyances.

It delegates authority to them: Instead of trying to bypass them, you are explicitly giving them the power to review the material first.

It establishes peer-level respect: It assumes the gatekeeper is smart enough to look at an executive summary (the dashboard snapshot) and determine its relevance to the company's current projects.

It usually results in a direct email: Nine times out of ten, a gatekeeper approached this respectfully will say, "Send it to me at [Direct Email], and if it looks relevant, I will forward it to [Decision Maker's Name]." You now have a warm, internal forward rather than a cold email.

## Leaving a Voicemail****

Keep it under 30 seconds, entirely focused on the "free value" you are trying to give them, and state that you will follow up via email.

**The Script**:

"Hi [Name], this is [Caller Name] from Practical Accessibility Ltd.I'm calling because we partner with digital healthcare providers to improve their digital accessibility, and we actually took the liberty of running a preliminary automated scan across 5 key pages of your platform using our custom analyzer.  I wanted to hand over the custom dashboard we built for you—it’s completely free and yours to keep. I’ll send a quick email with the PDF attached so you can take a look, and I'll try reaching you again next week. Thanks!"

# Telesales Script Guidance

## The Introduction & Hook

"Hi [Name], my name is [Caller Name] and I’m calling from Practical Accessibility Ltd. We are quality engineers who provide specialist accessibility audits for digital healthcare platforms like TympaHealth, Leva Clinic, and Asclepius. 

To introduce our services, we actually took the liberty of running a preliminary accessibility test across 5 key pages of your website using our custom analysis tools. I’m calling to see if you’d be open to receiving a free snapshot of those results?"

## The Core Value Pitch

"Most accessibility audits just hand engineering teams a massive, exhausting spreadsheet of isolated defects that can stall your roadmap. 

Practical Accessibility Ltd provides a much more practical approach. We layer deep manual testing with automated scans, and deliver prioritised findings with plain-English notes and developer-ready code fixes. 

We help teams understand WCAG standards without turning them into a box-ticking exercise. The free 5-page scan I have for you just gives you a quick baseline of where your platform stands today." 

## The Call to Action (The Ask)

"The preliminary dashboard is completely free and yours to keep. Would you or your engineering lead have 10 minutes next week for a quick screen share so one of our lead engineers can walk you through the high-level findings and see if our auditing services are a good fit for your roadmap?"

## Telesales Objection Handling

**Objection**: "We already have an internal QA team handling testing."

**Response**: "That’s completely fine. We regularly work alongside existing teams, providing specialist accessibility support during design and development. We can help unblock a stuck team, sense-check fixes, or simply provide a second layer of manual assistive technology testing before a major release." 

**Objection**: "We don't have the budget or time for a full accessibility audit right now."

**Response**: "I completely understand. That is exactly why we offer this free 5-page scan—it helps teams decide whether a full audit is even needed right now, or if just reviewing a specific feature makes more sense. It’s a great way to get a baseline without any commitment." 

**Objection**: "Our automated tools already catch our accessibility bugs."

**Response**: "Automated tools are a great start, but they only catch a portion of actual barriers. Our audits combine automated coverage with deep manual testing—including hands-on screen reader checks and evaluating mobile interaction patterns—which automated tools simply can't do accurately." 

**Objection**: "We use an accessibility overlay/plugin, so we are already compliant."
(This is a very common objection. Overlays do not fix the root code and often introduce new barriers).

**Response**: "That is exactly why I'm calling. Overlays can be a helpful temporary bandage, but they don't fix the underlying source code and often miss critical mobile interaction patterns or form validation errors. Our audits provide systemic analysis to help your team actually fix the root cause of these issues in your design patterns and components once and for all."  

**Objection**: "We are actually about to redesign our platform/app, so testing right now is a waste of time."

**Response**: "That is actually the perfect time to have this conversation. We specialize in early reviews—checking journeys, screens, components, and patterns before they are built and become expensive to change. We can provide hands-on help during your design and development phases so the new platform is accessible by default, rather than trying to fix it post-launch."  

**Objection**: "Our users haven't complained about accessibility."

**Response**: "That’s great to hear, though often users who encounter barriers simply leave the platform rather than taking the time to complain. In the healthcare sector, inclusive UX is a first-class quality concern. The free 5-page scan I want to share with you will just give you a factual, objective baseline of where your platform stands, completely free of charge, so you have the data when you need it."  

**Objection**: "We don't have the developer capacity to fix a bunch of accessibility bugs right now."

**Response**: "I completely understand, engineering time is always tight. That’s actually the core philosophy of Practical Accessibility Ltd. We don't just dump a list of defects on your team; we identify 'fix once, benefit many' opportunities. We group repeated issues so your team can make a single change to a shared component that instantly clears the issue across your entire site. It’s designed to save developer time, not drain it."  

**Objection**: "Just send me an email with some information."

**Response**: "I will absolutely do that. I'll send over the one-page overview of our practical audits, alongside the snapshot of the free test we ran on your site. If the dashboard highlights any systemic issues worth looking into, I'll give you a quick ring next week to see if you have 10 minutes to discuss them. What is the best email address to send this custom report to?"
